R84 cumulative Duixiu front cave, two independent fitted ascent routes and summit escape update. Run node start.mjs.
Includes all earlier R83 channel, R82 arch/pool, R81 water safety and R79 discovery documents.
The cave has a real closed rear wall and curved head clearance. Visible stone caps and stepped solids share non-rescue physical support. Two model routes measure approximately 27.2 and 27.4m, not surveyed historical path lengths.
A clearly labelled gameplay adaptation opens two south pavilion leaves and the rail passage; retained closed doors and rails are physical. The visible roof remains unchanged and is not reconstructed as a fully walkable roof.
CPU geometry, controller, navigation and night checks do not establish GPU/mobile appearance or natural-rock visual fidelity. This package does not deploy or merge main.
