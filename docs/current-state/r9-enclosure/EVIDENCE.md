## Bounded implementation

R8 duplicates eight southern lattice panels onto the northern flank. Replace those eight panels and their upper red strip portions with two physical solid wall flanks, red outside and pale inside. The one-metre lower band, colours and0.5m thickness are visual/model estimates. The existing north wall line, centre opening width, bay positions, hall dimensions and collision footprint are retained; none is newly asserted to be surveyed. No change to lighting, exposure, camera, core asset, current public-access claims or other halls.

The two walls contain28 triangles versus96 removed door-panel triangles, a net reduction of68. Their shared inner-finish material adds one material batch; exact whole-scene calls, triangles and memory still need final measurement. This reduction does not erase the separately recorded280-triangle coffer prototype. The combined geometric delta is+212 triangles before render passes. Original core and whole-scene performance limits are unchanged.
