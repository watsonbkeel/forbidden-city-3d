# Current-day connected western courts

Official sources read on 2026-10-18:
- 储秀宫 https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236486.html : late-Qing works removed Chuxiu Gate and its wall, connecting the palace with Yikun Palace. Short excerpt: “拆除了储秀门及围墙”.
- 长春宫 https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236436.html : Changchun Gate was removed in 1859 while the rear hall of Qixiang Palace became a through hall. Short excerpt: “拆除长春宫的宫门长春门”.
- 体和殿 https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236529.html : the former rear hall of Yikun Palace, five bays, hard-gable roof, with front/rear openings and a middle passage. Dining and tea spaces were at the sides.
- 体元殿 https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236544.html : the former rear hall of Qixiang Palace, five bays, hard-gable roof, with a middle passage; a north-facing theatre was attached behind it.

The old gates were not converted into these halls. Their existing model coordinates differ, and the official description supplies no modern surveyed coordinates. Both original gate points remain in search, cards and the map, explicitly marked as historical sites. Their physical gate bodies and collision boxes are removed. No ruins are invented. The two relevant rear halls keep their former model target names for navigation and now display the formal names 体和殿 and 体元殿 on their plaques.

Implemented topology: front/rear central openings, five bays and the generic gable family on these two halls; removal of the four artificial transverse boundaries between the two connected court pairs; four short perimeter joins using the prior estimated endpoints. All original building anchors, width, depth and height remain unchanged. Generic furniture is omitted from these through halls rather than placed in the verified middle passage. Detailed hard-gable end walls, ear rooms, theatre, room partitions and exact furnishings remain incomplete. This is a source-supported connection/silhouette correction, not complete measured reconstruction of these palace groups.

Focused CPU checks cover both directions through each hall and old gate site, solid outer joins, gate-marker rejection/zero geometry, map labels, formal plaque text, exact retained dimensions and only eight changed source objects plus four estimated joins. All 159 map cards also retain real target resolution and collision-clear landing after this correction. Native browser walkthrough and visual comparison remain part of the combined-release acceptance.
