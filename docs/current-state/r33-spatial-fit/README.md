## Qianqing

The Palace Museum confirms nine frontage bays including verandas, five depth bays, reduced front inner columns in the central bay, a throne/screen arrangement, side warm rooms and end through-passages. The official unscaled plan corroborates relationships, but does not establish every metric station.

- Official text: https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236472.html
- Xu Yilin, 后三宫, official article/plan: https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/pdf/2201/T00008_00.pdf
- Retained envelope: 46 × 25 m, centre (0,316.8), floor 2.16 m. These inherited values are not newly certified survey dimensions.
- Six fitted depth stations: −12.5, −9.5, −4.5, 4.5, 9.5, 12.5 m. The 3 m front/rear verandas and 5/9/5 m interior intervals are reversible model choices.
- 56 visible permanent red columns are the result of this model's omissions, not an official claim of total historic columns. The front central omission has official support; the second central omission retains the existing display's occupied volume and remains an estimated row identity.
- Front and rear facades sit on the inner veranda rows. The two end passages keep their original exterior mouths and connect to both verandas. Closed side leaves retain their labelled virtual pose.
- The optional detailed asset now owns floor only. Its two floor meshes and all 27 proxy bounds match R32 exactly; six obsolete optional columns/bases are removed. No loading state may double the structural grid.
- Central display, three stair groups, roof, caisson-free ceiling and original external routes retain their previous shape. The central display remains excluded from walking.

## Acceptance boundaries

Focused CPU tests cover real collision, doors, unchanged flight ceiling, optional-asset ownership, negative cases and disposal. They do not constitute native walking or photographic acceptance. Ordinary/controlled visual evidence and integrated budget results are recorded separately. No real-device smoothness or museum-authenticated reconstruction is claimed.
