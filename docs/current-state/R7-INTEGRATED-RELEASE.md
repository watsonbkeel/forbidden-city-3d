# R7 — Taihe interior and exterior approaches, 2026-10-18

## Visible changes

- The Taihe south terrace now has three staged levels with separate left/right stair flights, independent central imperial-stone strips and adjoining railings. The central stone strips are not treated as normal walkable stairs. Step counts, exact widths and heights remain modelling estimates pending better measured registration.
- The throne dais has three front approaches, thin separating rails and four tall open-legged incense stands with simplified tripod censers. The museum photograph and journal descriptions support their arrangement and identities. Individual material, decoration, exact dimensions and current temporary-cover status remain unverified.
- Six central transverse beam segments add depth using the existing column stations and materials.
- Huangji's S interior is aligned with the existing B rear opening. This is model registration, not a claim that the real rear clearance measures 0.95 m.

The rest of the palace and all 159 place descriptions remain available. Seven previously disclosed identity/history uncertainties are unchanged. This package does not claim that all palace stairs have already been individually corrected.

## Known visual limits

The interior is still stylized. The plaque/couplet mounting relationship, ceiling height/layers, screen details, urn materials and dark stair surfaces still need refinement against suitably registered evidence. Two lateral dais approaches need stronger registration evidence.

An apparent missing central road strip was reproduced in both R6 and R7 at exactly the same 1180×757 viewport, position/orientation/FOV and depth/material state. An older R6 image at 1188×761 did show it. This establishes that the defect is not introduced only by R7; it does not identify its rendering root cause or establish its incidence on other devices. No speculative road material or geometry modification is included.
