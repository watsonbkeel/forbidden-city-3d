# R48 NingShou architecture evidence

Read-only research completed 2026-10-21. No repository files modified. All images below are renderings or downloads of the linked official DPM sources. Source PDFs remain alongside screenshots. This report separates recorded topology from historical reconstruction and visual inference. No unmeasured numerical dimensions are supplied.

## 1. 符望阁: centered stairs, four radial rooms, north mezzanine

### Official 2025 article and first-floor plan
Source: 李越《耄期致勤倦 颐养谢尘喧：乾隆花园里的归去来兮》, 《紫禁城》2025年第4期.
https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/file/2025/04/23/1745375120FyrkQWemo260.pdf
- PDF page 11 / printed page 34: five bays on each side of ground floor, surrounding gallery; four radial 凸-shaped spaces around a four-direction throne core.
- PDF page 14 / printed page 37: first floor's intricate partitioned spaces contrasted with open top-floor space.
- PDF page 15 / printed page 38: unnumbered 《符望阁一层平面图》, 赵鹏绘, north arrow up, drawing label 1:100. Actual viewed plan shows a square five-by-five structural grid, surrounding gallery, cardinal entrance/stair axes, central stair compartment, four radial projections, and four inner diagonal compartment groups. It is not a random rectilinear maze or one open generic room.
Screenshots: fuwang-2025-p11.png, fuwang-2025-p14.png, fuwang-2025-p15.png; high-resolution plan crop fuwang-plan-detail.png.

### 2023 study supplies the missing vertical asymmetry and circulation
Source: 赵丛山《从符望阁壁钟看雍乾时期宫廷大墙表制作》, 《故宫博物院院刊》2023年第10期, pp.102–117.
https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2023/11/06/u654895df4c255.pdf
- PDF page 3 / printed 104 explicitly locates the stair room at the building center. The northern central room has an upper mezzanine/仙楼, giving it a lower ceiling than the south-facing main space. Four directions share the radial organization but have different decoration and furnishing themes.
- PDF page 4 / printed 105, figure 3: 《乾隆时期符望阁一层原状复原示意图》. This is a historical reconstruction diagram, not a 2026 furniture survey. Center stair enclosure and opposite short stair symbols are visible.
- East entrance's northern partition carries a two-sided wall clock; the clock and arched doors are observed features. A small south-facing theatre here is the author's evidence-based functional inference. Proposed route: north entrance, turn east into the backstage strip, pass an arched door to stage; opposite south subsidiary bay holds the audience location.
- Figure 2 on p104 specifically shows the north side of the clock wall before the 2014 repair. Do not restore its missing clock columns as current without newer evidence.
Screenshots: fuwang-clock-2023-p03.png, fuwang-clock-2023-p04.png; crop fuwang-plan-restoration-detail.png.

Implementation: use a central stair well, radial fixed partition topology, north-only lower mezzanine condition, and an open highest storey. Preserve the cardinal circulation. Mark furniture and theatre reconstruction as historical/inferred. Do not repeat the first-floor maze on all three floors. A complete measured middle/top-floor plan was not found, so exact slab cutouts, levels and stair dimensions remain model fits.

Exterior baseline: https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236495.html confirms square form, two visible/three actual levels, pyramidal roof, yellow glazed field with blue eave/ridge details.

## 2. 倦勤斋: east U-shaped 仙楼 and west theatre void

Primary building source:
https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236478.html
The nine-bay range is explicitly split into east five and west four. Eastern five has a 凹-shaped 仙楼, screen partitions and rooms, a central front entrance, and a connecting passage to the west. West has a bamboo-pattern square pavilion stage at the west end, facing east, and a two-level audience 仙楼 opposite, facing west. South circular opening-screen and north painted counterpart belong to the west interior. Rear stage doors serve entrances/exits. Front gallery is external; do not confuse it with the upper gallery.

Long rectangular range; theatre west, audience/transition at east end of theatre zone; east five bays have a rear line of small spaces and front open central area. 2. https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/pdf/fa/4f/9c/fa4f9c7a93bbb404521695f35043873e.pdf
Single-page PDF, printed p034, 《倦勤斋室内空间示意图一》《…二》. Local juanqin-cutaway.pdf / juanqin-cutaway-p01.png / juanqin-cutaway-detail.png. Actual viewed cutaways show the east U-shaped arrangement as a north/rear band with side returns, leaving the front-middle volume open. Western stage canopy remains within the tall theatre room; the raised audience block sits to its east. A stair beside the audience/transition block is visible. Implementation: do not make a full-width/full-depth second floor over the theatre. Keep stage, performance/audience gap and facing audience block legible. Build east U with a front-central void rather than three generic closed boxes. Preserve east-west connection and front gallery. Exact 仙楼 room counts, decorative inventories and stair dimensions are not fully verified.

## 3. 乐寿堂: two central double-height halls and a transverse upper passage

Primary source:
https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236470.html
Seven bays by three, surrounding gallery, 36.15m × 23.20m overall, 839m², partial upper floor 341m². Front four gold columns removed across main and side-center rooms; eight added support columns are described. East–west 阁道 divides north/south halls. South portions of outer side rooms and near-north-wall passage are single storey. Large Yu-the-Great jade mountain belongs to the northern hall. Do not replace this with a generic throne inventory.

### Distinctive partition supported by modern article/photo
https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/file/2025/02/13/1739432388igVFOKaLW260.pdf
PDF page 8 / printed p106, unnumbered 《乐寿堂一层平面图》 and photograph. Marks the fan-shaped 便面窗 in the partition between the front and rear spaces of the east 梢间 on the ground floor. Local leshou-2025-p08.png.

Implementation: an H-like partial upper-floor organization (side wings joined by transverse middle passage), not a full slab or simple U around one hall. Keep both central halls open above. Respect single-storey exceptions explicitly recorded by the building page; show the current jade mountain only in northern hall. Model detailed historic side-wing partitions with historical status, or retain uncertainty when targeting present condition.

## 4. Supplemental 养性殿 front 抱厦 check

The provided PDF https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2018/06/04/u5b14b610b0853.pdf is a 2016 paper uploaded in 2018. PDF page 6 / printed p60 figure 3 is a photograph of 养性殿. The large plan above it (figure 2) is the 养心殿 measured courtyard drawing, including its 水柱. Page 7 explicitly contrasts 养性殿, which lacks those 水柱. Do not cite figure 2 as 养性 measured plan. Screenshots yangxing-2018-p06.png and p07.png.

Verified official image URLs from the 养性殿 building page:
https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/Picture/2020/07/03/s5efeffe2c7b97.jpg
https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/Picture/2020/07/03/s5efefffe6a002.jpg
Local yangxing-s5efeffe2c7b97.jpg and yangxing-s5efefffe6a002.jpg.
Visual inference from these two views supports central three bays (narrow/wide/narrow, wide middle aligned with four-leaf entrance and stair) plus one west bay, not four equal bays. West extension has a red low screen/board. Trees obscure some west details, so this is a topology check, not measurement. The official building text confirms four front 抱厦 bays but does not itself state the exact 3+1 arrangement.

## Final supplement: 2012 Fuwang top-storey photograph

李越、王时伟《“迷楼”——符望阁》, 《紫禁城》2012年第4期, pp.10–23.
Official landing page https://www.dpm.org.cn/explode/others/252415.html explicitly links this PDF:
https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2020/04/22/u5ea0044616c59.pdf
Downloaded as fuwang-milou-2012.pdf (14 pages).
- PDF p1 / printed p10 explicitly specifies lower storey five bays with surrounding gallery, an intervening hidden storey, and upper storey three bays. Screenshot fuwang-milou-2012-p01.png.
- PDF p11 / printed p20 contains a photograph captioned 《符望阁三层全景》. Visually inspected: one open upper room, central square stair opening with wooden guardrail, perimeter doors/windows, visible ornamental roof structure. Screenshot fuwang-milou-2012-p11.png.
- PDF pages 3–8 / printed pp12–17 are literal placeholder pages marked 拉页 in the online file, not usable diagrams or interiors. No hidden-storey plan can be extracted from those placeholders.

Refined vertical conclusion: first storey + hidden/partial intermediate spaces + reduced three-bay open top storey, with central stair circulation. At least the north central first-floor bay has an upper mezzanine according to 2023 p104. The official youth guide https://young.dpm.org.cn/info/995 says multiple spaces have mezzanines. Therefore do NOT assert that the north patch is the sole mezzanine. A north-only modeled mezzanine is a conservative evidence-limited implementation, not a full reconstruction of every intermediate slab. Two complete intermediate five-by-five-bay slabs have no support. The precise hidden-storey floor outline and elevations remain unresolved.
