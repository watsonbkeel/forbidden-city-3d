# R48 third integration checkpoint

This is an intermediate integration backup, not a release or full-building acceptance. The current ledger has 52 of the 70 scoped identities classified and 45 with an actual change on at least one side. Eighteen identities remain pending. Interior and exterior statuses are separate.

## Corrections and evidence

- The 13 NingShou profiles now use actual FrontSide roof tops/undersides, closed custom shells and source-limited fitted bearing structures. Earlier detached roof assemblies, missing corner surfaces and the unsupported upper Biluo canopy were real defects, not snapshot failures. The original failing versions remain preserved. The final Biluo cap refinement shapes its real vertices to the sloping roof rather than leaving a flat cap protruding through it.
- Fifteen outer-region profiles are enabled. Only Cining's inferred throne overlay binding is retired in favor of the explicitly incomplete official sculpture-gallery panorama target. Its GLB/config remain unchanged for rollback and all five other live bindings are byte-equivalent to the R47 configuration.
- New whole-world consumer tests found a Baoxiang return endpoint too close to a neighboring platform for discrete controller steps. Moving only that fitted ground endpoint inward by 0.2 m fixes it without changing collision tolerances, structures or other entries. The original six failing movement modes are retained in the diagnostic history.
- Jiyun's actual north-turn approach is validated segment by segment before acceptance. Its unrelated direct sight chord crosses the neighboring platform; the exact Jiyun profile, like the existing Wenyuan bent approach, does not use that chord as the route. Negative tests still reject missing support or an obstruction on the real route.
- The residential rear-veranda default-radius clearance issue is not closed by this checkpoint. A separate fitted-depth/visible-stair correction and the expanded Yangxin exterior are being validated before integration. Earlier small-radius scans are not treated as player-clearance acceptance.
