# R84 coherent Duixiu mountain implementation plan

Goal: one integrated front vault cave, two independent ascent routes and safe summit exterior, preserving the upper pavilion rather than publishing an unfinished hollow mountain.

Architecture: a deterministic terraced solid profile owns all mass, cavity, step surfaces and collision/supports. A small bounded grid of closed stone cells makes a contiguous solid mountain and explicit shallow steps. Cells over the front cave have a real arched underside and existing piecewise-linear OverheadShape collision. Every support is rescue:false. Rendered stone tops and registered support tops share identical heights. The old generic rock generator remains untouched; only the GardenBuilder Duixiu instance opts into the new coherent assembly.

Approaches considered: cutting a visual hole through old random rocks cannot remove old solid cores or define safe stairs; a full surveyed reconstruction lacks measurements. Use a reversible deterministic stone mass with photo-supported cavity/ascent topology and openly fitted dimensions. User authorized fitting and autonomous inline implementation.

Constraints: center−29.1,509.1; original25.09×16.61 envelope and10m mountain/10.5 summit remain. Cave3m wide,4.5m deep,spring2m/crown3m,closed rear. No north through-hole, guessed inscriptions, fountains or hidden shafts. Existing roof/pillars/closed door appearance stays. Routes end outside the closed pavilion, not through door leaves. No global physics or layout relaxation.

Routes: independently fitted east/west front-to-side-to-rear-to-inner return lines, nominal1.8m tread width with small solid shoulders; length25..30m and <=.5 equivalent grade. Turn cells must support radius.5. An exterior summit apron connects both routes outside the inherited rail footprint. Real stone step bodies, not floating walkables. All other garden and NPC content retained as far as exact signatures permit; any unavoidable local nav/flight delta explicitly bounded and compared.

Quarter-metre closed cells share visible solids and physical support; only exactly adjacent equal-height physics rectangles are compacted. This is an engineering staircase interpretation, not surveyed stonework. Actual existing trees, beds, paths, summit meshes and non-mountain collider/support signatures remain unchanged.
