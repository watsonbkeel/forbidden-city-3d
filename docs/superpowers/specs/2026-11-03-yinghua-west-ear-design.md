# Yinghua west ear hall: bounded exterior fit

Implement H43 item 43, FC-BA2BB4F70D, as a south-facing three-bay yellow hard-gable ear hall in one east-west line with the retained five-bay Yinghua hall. Official building page, gallery 8420 and the measured-structure paper support the identity/topology. The ear hall's surveyed dimensions and hidden elevations are unknown.

Current public access and whole-object acceptance remain unverified.
