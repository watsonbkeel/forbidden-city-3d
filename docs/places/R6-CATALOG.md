# 159-place guide integration

Scope: 145 new entries plus the original 14 cards, which retain their complete objects and sources. Catalog totals 159 places and 129 distinct official source URLs. The original card/source bytes are recovered by an exact, bounded retention test; older historical fixtures were not replaced.

The seven real zone data files and main layout bind the cards. Nine Dragon Wall is explicitly resolved as a wall source record, even though its established map footprint uses the `hall` display category. Search accepts both existing model labels and five documented rear-hall names. Reading a card never teleports; travel remains a separate action, and returning to the map preserves the search.

Source limits are visible. Nanhun Gate's identity remains unresolved; Taiji Gate's name is a documented inference; two Changkang gates have only limited historical evidence; three Nansansuo rear halls have unresolved exact building correspondence. Chuxiu Gate and Changchun Gate are historical, demolished gates. Their retained coordinates are provisional old model points, not surveyed historical positions. No unverified independent function or date is invented to fill an empty section. Intended users, birthplaces, dining, worship and nearby confinement are not reclassified as residence.

`r6-source-review.json` contains the selected 145-entry provenance and short source excerpts; full retrieved pages and research caches are deliberately not distributed. Original modern-site access notices and current-day access disclaimers are preserved. Source facts establish identity/history, not dimensions, current visitor permission or architectural acceptance.

Verified locally: 217 focused catalog/map/guide-retention tests, including every one of the 159 entries through the real seven-zone registration, real Teleport target resolution and collision-backed landing. This is CPU/DOM simulation; browser layout, native map input and physical-device behaviour still require combined-release checks. Initial RED evidence and intermediate failures are retained outside the source checkout. No online publication was made for this component.
