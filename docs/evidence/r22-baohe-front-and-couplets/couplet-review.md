# Baohe pillar couplets: primary-source check
Reviewed 2026-10-18. Bounded read-only research; no repository changes, browser processes, tests, or publication.

## Confirmed wording and ordering
Each line has **15 main characters**, read vertically from top to bottom. Directions below mean a visitor looking toward the throne, as in the selected museum-contributed front-facing photograph.

- **Viewer-right / first line:** 祖訓昭垂我後嗣子孫尚克欽承有永
- **Viewer-left / second line:** 天心降鑒惟萬方臣庶當思容保無疆

The Palace Museum exhibition page supplies the normalized simplified text, separated into phrases: “祖训昭垂 我后嗣子孙 尚克钦承有永” / “天心降鉴 惟万方臣庶 当思容保无疆”. It labels the linked pair “乾隆皇帝御笔联”. Its HTML places `baohe-l` on the left and `baohe-r` on the right. The separately linked museum specimen photographs were downloaded and visually inspected; they directly support the traditional transcription above, including **後** and **鑒**. This is not an OCR-only reconstruction. Preserve 惟 (not 唯). Ordinary typeface rendering of these characters would still be an approximation of the photographed calligraphy. Do not invent small seal inscriptions.

## Selected photograph: visible placement only
Source: https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/hall-of-preserving-harmony-baohe-dian-interior-the-palace-museum/pgFXua5sp2QCug?hl=zh-cn ; local `museum-contributed-interior-s1200.jpg` (1200×800), already documented in `source-review.md`.

Both narrow yellow-field boards have black writing, red edge accents and darker outer frames. They flank the screen/throne axis, inward of the large foreground red pillars. Their tops sit approximately level with the lower edge of the horizontal 皇建有極 plaque; lower portions are partly obscured by furnishings near platform-rail height. Rough manual projected outer-frame envelopes: left x400–442, y280–606; right x764–807, y280–607. These include frame, have several-pixel uncertainty, and are not perspective-corrected. Approximate projected outer width/height is 0.13; the yellow writing field is narrower. Do not convert this into measured meters, board thickness, attachment details, or a survey claim.

## What is not established
Neither the selected photograph nor the exhibition/specimen pages establish the photographic capture date, installation/restoration date, original-versus-reproduction status, or the version currently displayed in October 2026. The exhibition's author attribution is not a dating certificate for the photographed physical boards. The official building page (https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236434.html), reviewed alongside the existing note, does not close those gaps. Use the explicit label “based on an undated museum-supplied interior photograph,” not “verified current display” or “original Qing installation.” No new current-state assertion is justified by a URL/page timestamp.
