# Writer inspection of final cumulative World CPU views

Personally viewed `cpu-world-0819/whole.png`, `end.png`, and `panel.png` after the exporter reached exit0. Whole view contains all three production bridges and the retained gates/banks; no neighbour was hidden for presentation. End view exposes the shallow beveled base beneath the retained curved end; panel view shows the narrow base/shaft seat. The material surfaces are visibly pale CPU functional/base colors, not runtime stone textures, lighting or GPU output.

These images do not prove surveyed widths, individual carved ornament, hidden bridge vault/underside, historical counts, 2026 condition or whole-object fidelity. The close panel view looks across water from outside the bridge edge; absence of a depicted measured underside is not accepted or silently filled with invented geometry. Existing `wholeObjectAccepted=false` and source-fit dimensions remain.
