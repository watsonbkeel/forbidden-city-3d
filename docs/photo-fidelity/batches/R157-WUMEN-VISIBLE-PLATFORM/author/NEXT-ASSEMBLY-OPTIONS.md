## Approaches

## Design decisions still requiring review
1. Platform footprint: the prior1632 ±63/inner39/south−96/north14.5 numbers are NOT accepted dimensions. Main/wing outer extent, northern bearing width, courtyard inner face and south end must be chosen together with actual column feet, masonry silhouette and five portals. Retaining existing inner face40.6 may preserve validated side-door alignment, but cannot be silently declared source-confirmed. OSM lower contour is a reference, not the top boundary.
2. Ascent/platform overlap: either use a source-supported recess, or explicitly mark the necessary visible stepped/ramped recess as engineering fitting. Do not authorize a hidden cut based only on `map-layer.json`. Topology must avoid coplanar duplicate support and buried ramp surfaces. The receiver region becomes part of a named platform mesh only when the corresponding real closed geometry exists; it is not accepted by renaming the old test block.
3. Pavilion scale: new north and south reference envelopes use each side's own plan registration. No blanket scaling of old body/roof/material UV/collider is approved. Preserve or explicitly re-register column radii, eaves, facade panels and old independently observed details. Eight corner interface shaft areas stay unresolved, not a fabricated8-post claim.
4. Initial route target: actual exposed platform, if a clear path exists outside the closed pavilion body. This is not pavilion entry. If no such physical path exists, report the blockage rather than tunnelling through the retained solid box. To open a pavilion requires a separate visible enclosure/doorway contract and exact actual colliders.
5. Lower five portals: preserve their individual geometry, frame/leaf/cap/side-face content and transforms under only an approved rigid/face-attachment change. New footprint cannot detach a mouth from its masonry or retain an invisible closed backing. Main tower1.43 grid/roof and all current accepted details remain explicitly bounded preservation targets.
6. Walls: no globalPALACE_RECT move. South long segments/side walls/corners and ascent cap interfaces require named target mapping; the new main origin cannot silently disable the original-anchor guards. Que gates remain unchanged.
