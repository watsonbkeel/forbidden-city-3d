## Textual type and present-existence evidence

- [DPM 南三所](https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236452.html): three courts / three stages, front halls three bays, middle/rear five; separate courtyard gates and overall green glazed roofs. Its condensed wording about hard-gable roofs does not individually resolve the first-row roof exception.
- [建筑史学刊 editor-authenticated primary post, 2024-09-03](https://www.sina.cn/news/detail/5074467804153118.html), citing 王藏博《故宫南三所一进院正殿年代考》2024, 5(2):122–129: explicitly distinguishes the three first-court halls as three-bay single-eave **xieshan**, other buildings hard-gable. Publisher's text was read via indexed full result; this audit did NOT obtain/view the article's individual author photographs or use their fine details. Official aerial/map independently support the coarse type.
- [2025 Beijing Cultural Heritage Bureau response](https://wwj.beijing.gov.cn/bjww/362679/362680/482911/743618390/index.html): current protection/repair design for the middle compound; not a completion report, visitor-access permission or exact footprint registry.

## Do not confuse missing model with demolished building

Original 12 IDs remain: aggregate region + common gate + nine principal-hall tasks + unresolved 撷芳 record. Current six declarations are three front-labeled south-row shells (12/14/16), two rear-labeled shells (13/17) and the unresolved 撷芳 shell (15). The three middle-hall IDs and separate middle-rear ID are unbound, NOT four automatically missing new buildings. No exact middle/rear mapping is claimed here.

[李燮平《从乾隆朝养心殿的改造看明清紫禁城硬山建筑的变化》p142](https://www.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2020/11/12/u5facb56c07c8f.pdf), SHA256 `3836ed20880121a3d711a94635aaf440cd9762aaac8279b116a98029aebbc841`, personally read/viewed: 1950s demolition refers to the gate-front ancillary six-room structures, NOT wholesale demolition of the three hall groups. [DPM stove research](https://img.dpm.org.cn/Uploads/File/2020/11/13/u5fae21c07c127.pdf) says middle-hall stoves were removed and east-middle kang remains sealed; no stove or inferred ancient furniture is added.

## Engineering limits

All centers, 15.4×10.6m body envelope, 0.9m original floor/steps and 5.04m columns are inherited estimates. Three bays, 2.65m fitted central passage, four-meter roof rise, plain green/red/timber hues and virtual open leaves are reversible engineering fits, not surveys or photographic measurements. The plain flank/back/ceiling closures do not claim actual window panels, a painted ceiling or hidden north decoration. Roof-beast census, detailed bracket type, carving, fine lattice, actual interior inventory and present visitor access remain unverified. No current unidentified rear shell is altered and no third row is created.
