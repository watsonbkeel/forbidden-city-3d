# 坤宁门 FC-60FCD2B8CC, bounded R61 correction

This is a partial model-fit checkpoint, not acceptance of the whole gate or D32.
Official source: https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236582.html . All three
page photos were actually inspected. Their URL date is 2020-04-20; capture dates
are unknown. No source image is redistributed in this repository or production.

## Source orientation and actual difference

The official text identifies a north-facing three-bay gate, with solid walls on
the north side bays and lattice windows at the rear/south. Therefore the page's
first windowed photo is the SOUTH/rear, despite its nonspecific “坤宁门” caption.
The tree-obscured oblique view is the NORTH/front. The close-up confirms a vertical
blue/gold nameboard inside a red ribbon frame. Guardroom interiors are unverified.

The old actual production model was a generic south-facing hall gate. Its width
marker 12 became a 13.6 m central bay in a 20.8 m body, with no south windows,
no real stairs, and its only nameboard facing south. Before CPU views preserve it.

The new explicit north-facing profile keeps the world registration (1.1,422.7),
body 20.8×6.4 m, outer wall gap 20.8 m, platform 22.4×8 m and 6.2 m column height.
It fits three bays as 6.24/8.32/6.24, north masonry/south three-field lattice,
recessed single paired doors, and a photo-supported stair/centre-stone relationship.
The base rises from .45 to 1.2 m. Eight .15 m risers over 3.2 m, stair widths and
all proportions are REVERSIBLE FITS, not measured or documentary dimensions.
A plain centre stone omits all unverified carving; modern visitor fences are not
misrepresented as permanent building fabric. The practical route uses side treads.

The simplified red ribbon is original geometry with system-font Chinese text.
It is NOT a trace of the photograph, original calligraphy or a verified bilingual
copy. The Manchu inscription is explicitly pending, not invented. Complete glazed
screen wings, attached gallery interfaces, roof/ceiling detail, guardroom joinery,
interiors and accurate fine paint remain required residuals. Rooms are not falsely
registered as known empty or supplied with generic invented furniture.

## Exact scope and checks

Production: one new KunningGate.js, one import/opt-in branch in CourtKit, and three
fields on the existing garden registration. All other CourtKit source bytes and
all other garden declarations are checked against frozen R60. Physics, character
registry/Kit, shared controls and Teleport are unchanged. Navigation is deliberately
changed by the real raised base. Historical .45 m/ground-only Kunning assertions
are retained as obsolete expectations, not edited to pass or counted as passes.
QA route consumers now use the explicit side-stair chain before returning to the
retained 钦安殿 eastern ground-view route. No other temple is added to the scope.

15 focused checks pass, including eight 10/60 Hz × W/S × walk/sprint round trips,
actual triangle supports, non-target retention, negative invalid/closed profiles,
and merged FC identity. Ten wall-connected fragment checks additionally pass,
including real body clearance every recorded movement frame and unchanged actual
Teleport's safe north landing. The fragment contains the enclosing wall and its
three referenced gates, not a whole garden/world; whole-world D validation remains
pending. The earlier red/logs are preserved, including two harness mistakes:
unknown wood_red material name and a Node JSON import hook omission. Both fixed
without weakening production behavior. A ray aimed through a lattice opening was
corrected to test the real frame, and the centre stone is checked as an actual
existing ramp primitive instead of incorrectly demanding every support be a box.

Geometry: 1790 -> 3724 triangles (+1934), before/final visual geometry hashes and
cameras in reports/r61-d-stage/{before,kunning-profile-01}/kunning-gate.json .
Metadata-only identity/support additions follow that image capture; static geometry
is unchanged. CPU images are actual local geometry with real albedo/UV, but omit
WebGL PBR, normal maps, shadows and tone mapping. They are NOT browser/GPU/mobile or
whole-world acceptance. Source images remain private and linked by URL/hash only.
