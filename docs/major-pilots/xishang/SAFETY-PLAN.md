## Proven failure and rejected alternatives

Reconstructed archived rejected candidate **in memory only** against 9e4159c. At 10/60 Hz a 9 m drop stays airborne near feet 5.05 m. Ordinary support uses a circular foot query while vertical collision sweeps the actual square body. A square-contact-only hypothesis lands, but 20/36 paths penetrate source triangles and 10 fail to leave: horizontal authoritative feet and ground smoothing also matter. Do not globally patch these algorithms or add epsilons.

5. Strict guards reject unknown IDs, missing/duplicate anchors, changed source transforms/buffers, removed floors and out-of-domain witness claims. All old object paths remain observationally identical.
