## Visual and runtime boundaries

`visuals/*.png` are six actual integrated-scene CPU perspective z-buffer captures, not isolated concepts or browser/GPU screenshots. Base/vertex colors are present, but textures, runtime lights, shadows and postprocessing are not reproduced. Pale buildings in these diagnostic images do not mean game textures were removed; absent contact shadows can make a correctly supported sole look detached. Real individual sole triangles were checked separately. Male eunuch walking keeps the previously approved template's front-hand silhouette; this is not a newly authored natural arm-swing style.

Shoukang has no S GLB in the registry. Its procedural B framework is checked before/while Yangxin S is loaded; that is not a claim of Shoukang-S reconstruction. Yangxin S is actual checked-in GLB geometry decoded with the checked-in Draco CPU path and the real InteriorLoader lifecycle; browser texture loading/GPU rendering is not exercised.

GPU frame-time/draw-call performance, WebGL visual parity, physical-phone experience and full historical validation remain unverified. CPU geometry checks are not substituted for them. People remain default-on as requested, with a one-click settings off switch and formal-acceptance status false. The release/build label and copied diagnostic distinguish registered/ready/currently-visible/pending/error counts. No automatic refresh, cache deletion or user-data deletion occurs.
