## Historical scope

The official Palace Museum building pages establish broad uses, not these staff identities, numbers, exact duties or schedules:

- Yangxing Hall: https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236458.html
- Leshou Hall: https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236470.html
- Jingfu Palace: https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236571.html
- Chonghua Palace: https://www.dpm.org.cn/explore/building/236506.html

Retirement plans do not establish that Qianlong actually occupied these halls. Jingfu's1689 use and1772 rebuilding belong to different historical stages. Chonghua's princely residence and later palace/tea-banquet uses are not a single daily routine. No strong claim that Qianlong never resided is inferred from these pages. The specific actors, gender/role assignment, placement and animation are an explicitly switchable interpretive layer, not current museum workers or a synchronized dated reconstruction.

Chonghua's full official page was read. For the other three, official search text was retrieved; direct full-page reads timed out. No new clothing or image-based reconstruction is claimed. Yangxing, Leshou and Jingfu points are existing modeled hall-front spaces, not evidence of fully reconstructed independent courtyards.
